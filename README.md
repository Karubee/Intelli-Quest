# Intelli-Quest

**Intelli-Quest** is a web-based interactive learning game designed to improve student engagement and learning outcomes by integrating gamified review content with turn-based combat mechanics. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI), the system transforms scanned documents into interactive quiz questions, enabling students to engage in competitive quiz-based battles while enhancing their knowledge retention and performance.

---

## **How It Works**

1. **User Account Creation**: Players create or log into an account to save progress and access features.
2. **PDF Upload**: Players scan and upload learning materials. The system uses AI to extract key information and generate review questions.
3. **Turn-Based Quiz Battles**: Players answer quiz questions derived from their uploaded materials to defeat AI-controlled enemies. Faster responses deal more damage, making gameplay dynamic and rewarding.
4. **Difficulty Levels**: Questions adapt based on chosen difficulty levels: Easy (True/False), Medium (Multiple Choice), or Hard (Identification).
5. **Customizable Settings**: Players can adjust settings such as the number of enemies, time limits, and difficulty.
6. **Flashcard Creation**: The system generates flashcards from uploaded PDFs, allowing students to study at their own pace.
7. **Real-Time Feedback**: Players receive instant feedback on their answers to enhance learning.
8. **Performance Tracking**: A scoreboard tracks player progress and high scores, encouraging improvement.

---

## **Key Features**

### 1. **User Account Module**
- **Register Account**: Allows users to create a new account to save progress.
- **Account Log-In**: Prompts users to input credentials to access personalized features.

### 2. **PDF Uploader Module**
- Upload scanned learning materials.
- AI processes the uploaded documents to generate quiz questions.

### 3. **Turn-Based Quiz Battles**
- Engage in battles with AI-controlled enemies using quiz questions derived from uploaded materials.
- Gameplay adapts to three levels of difficulty:
  - **Easy**: True/False questions.
  - **Medium**: Multiple-choice questions.
  - **Hard**: Identification-based questions.
- Faster answers inflict more damage to enemies, adding a competitive edge.

### 4. **Difficulty Adjustment**
- Customizable difficulty levels based on player progress.
- Adjustments include the number of enemies and time limits per question.

### 5. **Customization Options**
- Players can modify game settings to suit their preferences and skill levels.

### 6. **Flashcard Creation**
- Automatically generates flashcards from uploaded PDFs.
- Provides an alternative way for players to review and study materials.

### 7. **PDF Collection**
- Repository for storing past PDFs.
- Players can reuse saved study materials for future battles or flashcards.

### 8. **Scoreboard**
- Tracks player high scores for each PDF and difficulty level.
- Encourages progress by showcasing improvements.

### 9. **Subject Variety**
- Supports various academic subjects (e.g., English, History) to cater to different learning needs.

### 10. **AI-Controlled Enemies**
- Dynamic and challenging AI enemies adapted to difficulty levels.
- Players must defeat enemies by answering quiz questions correctly and quickly.
